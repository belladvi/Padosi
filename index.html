Questions

Good to know.

01How do I pay?

You pay the cook directly over UPI. Padosi organises the live menu and the order list — payment and pickup stay between you and your neighbour.

02Pickup or delivery?

Pickup, a few floors away. Because the cook is inside your own society, there's no delivery wait and no delivery fee.

03Is the food hygienic?

It's cooked fresh in a neighbour's home kitchen and sealed before it leaves. You're ordering from someone in your own building, not an anonymous kitchen.

04I'm a cook — how do I start?

List today's dishes, portions and a cut-off in under a minute, then share your link to the society group. That's the whole setup.
